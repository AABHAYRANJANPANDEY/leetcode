## 🛠️ Key Capabilities & Tools

Microsoft Power Platform consists of five core pillars:

1. **Power Apps:** Build custom, responsive web and mobile applications with low-code pre-built templates and custom logic.
2. **Power Automate:** Create automated workflows between your favorite apps and services to synchronize files, get notifications, and collect data.
3. **Power BI:** Turn raw data into actionable dashboards and interactive business intelligence reports.
4. **Power Pages:** Rapidly design, configure, and publish modern, secure enterprise web applications for external users.
5. **Microsoft Copilot Studio:** Create custom AI-driven conversational assistants tailored to your data and enterprise logic.
